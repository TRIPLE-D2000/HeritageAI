Set up Heritage AI in about 10 minutes

Heritage AI is not on the Google Play Store yet, so Android needs a few extra taps the first time. Follow the steps in order and tick each one as you go.

Heritage AI controls your phone for you, so Android asks for each permission. They are all optional, but the assistant can only do what you allow.

Tips for your phone

Phone makers add their own battery rules that can switch Heritage AI off in the background. Open yours and follow it. Menu names vary a little between versions.

Samsung (One UI)
  • Switch Auto Blocker off while installing: Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker. Turn it on again afterwards.
  • Settings → Apps → Heritage AI → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Settings → Battery → Background usage limits: remove Heritage AI from “Sleeping apps” and “Deep sleeping apps”.
Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)
  • Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Heritage AI → Autostart: on.
  • Same page, Battery saver → No restrictions.
  • Open recent apps, press and hold the Heritage AI card and tap the lock icon so it isn’t cleaned.
  • In Other permissions allow Display pop-up windows while running in the background and Show on lock screen.
  • The volume triple-press can be blocked on some versions. Use the in-app mic or wake word if so.
Tecno, Infinix and itel (HiOS / XOS / itel OS)
  • Open Settings → Apps → Heritage AI → Battery and choose No restrictions or Unrestricted.
  • Look in Settings (or the phone-manager app) for Auto-start or Background activity and allow Heritage AI.
  • In recent apps, lock Heritage AI so it isn’t closed to free memory.
  • Menus differ slightly between models. If you get stuck, message me and tell me your phone model.
Oppo, Realme and OnePlus (ColorOS / OxygenOS)
  • Settings → Battery → App battery management → Heritage AI: allow background activity and auto launch; turn off sleep or deep sleep for it.
  • Lock the app in recent apps.
vivo and iQOO (Funtouch / OriginOS)
  • Settings → Battery → Background power consumption management → Heritage AI → Allow.
  • Settings → Apps → Autostart: enable Heritage AI.
Google Pixel and other stock Android
  • Settings → Apps → Heritage AI → App battery usage → Unrestricted.
Force-stopping an app, or letting the system force-stop it, switches its Accessibility permission off. If the triple-press stops working one day, open Settings → Accessibility → Heritage AI and switch it back on.

Fix a problem

It says “App not installed” or Play Protect blocked it
  • Switch off Scan apps with Play Protect (Play Store → profile → Play Protect → gear), then install again.
  • On Samsung, also switch off Auto Blocker.
  • If it says the app conflicts with an existing package, uninstall the old Heritage AI first and then install the new one. Your chats on the old version will be removed.
  • Make sure the file finished downloading. A very small or partial file will fail.
My phone says it isn’t allowed to install unknown apps
Tap Settings on that message and switch on Allow from this source for Google Drive or Chrome (whichever opened the file). Then go back and tap Install. You can also find it at Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps.
The Accessibility switch won’t turn on
This is Android’s “restricted settings” protection for apps installed outside the Play Store. Open Settings → Apps → Heritage AI, tap ⋮ (top right), choose Allow restricted settings, confirm with your fingerprint or PIN, then try again. If the three dots aren’t there, tap the greyed-out switch inside the app once first and look again.
The triple volume press does nothing
  • Check that Accessibility → Heritage AI is switched on.
  • Set battery to Unrestricted and lock the app in recent apps (see your phone’s tips above).
  • Press quickly, three times, with the screen on.
  • Switch Accessibility off and on again, then retry.
  • On some Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones the system blocks this shortcut. Use the in-app mic or the wake word instead.
It says “No internet connection” but my data is working
  • Make sure you have the latest version of Heritage AI from the download page. Older versions can fail to connect.
  • Try another network (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • Open Settings → Network → Private DNS and set it to Automatic (or dns.google), then try again.
Brightness won’t change
Open Settings → Apps → Heritage AI → Change system settings and switch Allow permission on. If it’s greyed out, use Allow restricted settings from the three-dot menu first (see the Accessibility steps), then try again. Say a number, like “set brightness to 40”.
“Call Mum” or “text Ade” doesn’t work
  • In Heritage AI go to Settings and tap Contacts & Calling and allow both permissions.
  • Say the name the way it’s saved in your contacts. If two people match, Heritage will ask which one.
  • Texts open your messages app with the message already typed. Tap Send to confirm.
  • If the name isn’t an exact match, Heritage opens the dialer instead of calling straight away, so you can check the number.
It doesn’t hear me, or hears itself talking
  • Allow the Microphone permission.
  • Speak close to the phone in a quiet place.
  • Press the mic or triple-press again while it is talking. It will stop speaking and start listening.
The wake word or shortcut stops working after a while
Your phone is closing the app to save battery. Set Battery to Unrestricted, allow Autostart, and lock the app in recent apps. See the tips for your phone above.
Is it safe? Why did I have to turn off Play Protect?
Heritage AI isn’t on the Google Play Store yet, so Play Protect doesn’t recognise it and warns about it. Turn it back on straight after installing. Only install the file from the official Heritage AI download page. Your chats are stored encrypted on your phone, your contact list is never uploaded, and you can erase everything any time in Settings → Legal & Privacy → Delete all my data. When the app reaches the Play Store you won’t need any of these extra steps.
How do I update Heritage AI?
Download the newest file from the website and install it. If Android says it conflicts with the installed one, uninstall the old version first and install the new one.
How do I delete my data or uninstall?
Open Heritage AI → Settings → Legal & Privacy → Delete all my data to erase your chats, memories and settings. Then uninstall like any other app.
